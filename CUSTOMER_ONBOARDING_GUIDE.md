## 1. What Nashr is — ما هو «نشر»

Nashr is a place to plan, write, approve and schedule your social media posts —
for one business, or for many.

Instead of posting to Instagram, then Facebook, then TikTok, one at a time, from
your phone, at 11pm: you write the post once in Nashr, choose which accounts it
goes to and when, someone approves it, and Nashr publishes it for you at the
right time.

**What Nashr is good at:**

- Writing one post and sending it to several accounts at once
- Planning a whole week or month ahead on a calendar
- Getting a post approved — by your manager, or by your client — **before** it
  goes live
- Writing in both Arabic and English, adapted for the local market rather than
  translated word-for-word
- Ready-made campaign plans for Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, UAE National
  Day, Saudi National Day and Saudi Founding Day

**What Nashr does not do** — please read section 9 before you start. There are
real gaps and we would rather you hear them from us now than discover them in
week two.

## 2. Before you begin — قبل أن تبدأ

Have these ready:

| You need | Why |
|---|---|
| A business email address | Your Nashr login |
| The logins for your own social accounts | To connect them (see section 4 — you will **not** give us your passwords) |
| An Instagram **Business or Creator** account, linked to a Facebook Page | Instagram does not allow scheduled posting from personal accounts. This is Instagram's rule, not ours. |
| Your business details | Name, what you do, who your customers are, your tone of voice |
| Anything you are **not allowed to say** | Especially important for clinics and anything health-related — see section 3 |

**One thing to know now:** connecting Instagram, Facebook and TikTok requires
approval from those platforms, and approval can take **one to three weeks**.
This is entirely outside our control. We will start those applications on your
first day. Until they are approved, you can still write and plan everything —
you just cannot publish to those channels yet.

## 3. Setting up your business profile — ملف نشاطك التجاري

This is the most valuable 10 minutes you will spend in Nashr. Everything the
system writes for you is based on this.

You will be asked for:

- **What kind of business you are** — restaurant, agency, salon, clinic, or
  small business. Nashr has different content ideas for each.
- **Where you are** — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Egypt or
  Jordan. This changes the tone, the calendar and the local references.
- **Your tone of voice** — friendly and casual, or formal and professional?
- **Who your customers are** — families, young professionals, tourists, other
  businesses?
- **What you sell** — dishes, treatments, services, products.
- **Things you must never claim** — this one matters.

### Things you must never claim — العبارات الممنوعة

Nashr lets you list phrases the system is **forbidden** from writing. Every
piece of text it generates is checked against your list before you ever see it.

Examples of what people put here:

- A clinic: *"cure", "guaranteed results", "painless", "no side effects", "best
  doctor"*
- A restaurant: *"100% organic"* (unless certified), *"the best in Dubai"*
- A salon: *"permanent", "risk-free"*

**Why this exists:** advertising rules in the UAE and Saudi Arabia are strict,
and health-related claims are stricter still. This list is your safety net. Take
it seriously — it is much easier than fixing a published post.

## 4. Connecting your social accounts — ربط حساباتك

### How it works, and why it is safe

You connect each account by clicking "Connect", which sends you to that
platform's **own** login page — Instagram's page, TikTok's page, LinkedIn's
page. You log in there, and that platform asks whether you want to give Nashr
permission to post on your behalf.

**We never see, ask for, or store your social media passwords.** Ever. If any
tool ever asks you to type your Instagram password into *their* screen, close
it — that is not how this works and it is not safe.

You can remove Nashr's permission at any time from the platform's own settings,
without asking us.

### What you can connect

The main ones for businesses in this region:

- Instagram (Business or Creator account)
- Facebook Pages
- TikTok
- X (Twitter)
- LinkedIn — personal profiles and company pages
- YouTube
- Threads
- **Google Business Profile** — worth doing. For a restaurant, salon or clinic,
  this is what customers see when they search for you on Google Maps.
- Pinterest, Telegram, and about two dozen others

### 🔴 Snapchat is not available

**Nashr cannot post to Snapchat.** Not now, and not soon.

We are telling you this before you sign up, in bold, because we know Snapchat is
big here — especially for restaurants, salons and clinics. If Snapchat is
central to how you reach customers, Nashr will only do part of your job, and you
should decide with that in front of you.

You can still post to Snapchat manually, as you do today. Nashr simply will not
do it for you.

If Snapchat matters to you, please say so. We are building the beta partly to
find out how many people it blocks.

### When a channel stops working

Occasionally a connected account stops working — you changed your password, or
the platform expired the connection. You will see the channel marked as needing
attention. Reconnecting takes about 30 seconds and works exactly like connecting
it the first time. Nothing is lost.

## 5. Your team and who can approve — فريقك والصلاحيات

Nashr has six kinds of user:

| Role | What they can do | Typical person |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing | You |
| **Admin** | Everything except billing | Your marketing manager |
| **Editor** | Write and edit posts, but **cannot publish** | Your content person, a freelancer |
| **Approver** | Review and approve posts | A manager |
| **Client** | See and approve **only their own brand's** posts | Your client, if you are an agency |
| **Viewer** | Look, but change nothing | An intern, a stakeholder |

**If you are an agency:** each client gets their own brand inside your workspace.
A Client user sees only their own brand — never your other clients. Keep it that
way; it is the point.

## 6. How approval works — كيف تتم الموافقة

This is the part that makes Nashr different from posting from your phone.

```
   Draft            Internal review        Client approval        Scheduled          Published
   مسودة        →     مراجعة داخلية      →      موافقة العميل     →      مجدول        →      منشور
```

1. **Draft** — someone writes the post. Nobody outside sees it.
2. **Internal review** — they send it to a manager, who approves it or asks for
   changes.
3. **Client approval** — if you are an agency, your client sees it and approves
   it. (You can turn this step off if you do not need it.)
4. **Scheduled** — approved posts go into the calendar at the time you chose.
5. **Published** — Nashr posts it. You get a notification.

At any point someone can **request changes** or **reject** the post. It goes back
to the writer with a note. Every decision is recorded, with who made it and when,
so "who approved this?" always has an answer.

### The promise we make about this

**A post that has not been approved cannot be published.** Not by accident, not
by a mistake in the app, not through any back door.

We built this check into the part of the system that actually does the
publishing — not into the buttons on the screen. This matters: it means the rule
holds no matter how the post was created.

### About the AI: it will never post for you

Nashr can suggest captions, plan a month of content, adapt English to Arabic and
summarise your results. It is genuinely useful.

**But it cannot publish, delete, schedule or change anything on its own.**

When the AI wants to do something that changes your account, it stops and shows
you exactly what it proposes to do. You click approve. Only then does it happen —
and only exactly what you saw. If anything about the proposal changes, your
approval no longer counts and it asks again.

**Automatic publishing is switched off, and it starts switched off.** Someone
with the right permissions has to deliberately turn it on. We recommend leaving
it off.

## 7. Your first post — أول منشور لك

1. Open the **calendar** and click the day and time you want.
2. Choose which accounts it goes to. You can pick several at once.
3. Write your post — or click the AI assistant and describe what you want.
   ("A post about our new Ramadan iftar menu, warm and family-focused, in
   Arabic and English.")
4. Add a photo or video.
5. Check how it will look — Nashr shows a preview for each platform, because
   what fits on X does not fit on Instagram.
6. Send it for review.
7. Once approved, it sits in the calendar until its time comes.

**Times are in your local timezone.** The default is Dubai time (GST). If your
business is elsewhere, change it in Settings first — before you schedule
anything.

## 8. Arabic and English — العربية والإنجليزية

You can use Nashr in English or Arabic. Switch language at any time from the top
menu; the whole layout flips to right-to-left for Arabic.

For your **content**, Nashr does something better than translating: it *adapts*.
A caption written for Dubai is rewritten for Riyadh with the right references and
tone, rather than translated word-for-word. You can ask for a literal translation
if you specifically want one.

### Please read this about Arabic quality

The Nashr **interface** — the buttons, menus and labels — was translated
automatically by a machine and **has not yet been checked by a native Arabic
speaker**. Around 700 phrases are in this state.

Most of it will be fine. Some of it will read awkwardly. A few things may be
plainly wrong.

We are telling you rather than hoping you do not notice. A native Arabic review
is underway and is a condition of our full launch.

**Please tell us when you see bad Arabic.** Screenshot it and send it. You are
the reason it will get fixed, and your corrections go to a native reviewer, not
back into a machine.

One more thing you will notice: **error messages are still in English**, even
when you are using Nashr in Arabic. If something goes wrong — a post fails, a
file is rejected — that message has not been translated yet. We know. It is on
the list.

## 9. What Nashr will not do — ما لا يقوم به «نشر»

Please read this section before you commit. We would rather lose a signup than
have a disappointed customer in week three.

| | |
|---|---|
| ❌ **Snapchat** | Not supported. See section 4. This is the biggest gap. |
| ⚠️ **Arabic interface quality** | Machine-translated, under review. See section 8. |
| ⚠️ **Error messages in Arabic** | Still English only. |
| ❌ **WhatsApp** | No WhatsApp Business messaging or automation. |
| ❌ **Customer database / CRM** | Nashr manages your posts, not your customers. |
| ❌ **Replying to comments and DMs** | You still do this on each platform. |
| ❌ **Mobile app** | Nashr works in a phone browser, but there is no app to install. |
| ⚠️ **Analytics** | Available for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, LinkedIn Pages, YouTube, Threads, Google Business Profile and Pinterest. **Not** for Telegram, personal LinkedIn, or most smaller platforms — those will show no numbers. |
| ⚠️ **Calendars outside the UAE** | The calendar currently starts the week on Monday. Correct for the UAE. **Not correct for Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Egypt or Jordan**, where the week starts Sunday. We are fixing this; for now, double-check dates when you schedule. |
| ⚠️ **Hijri calendar** | Ramadan and Eid campaign dates are calculated correctly, but the calendar you look at is Gregorian. |
| ⚠️ **Platform approval delays** | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Google Business Profile all require their own approval before publishing works. One to three weeks, sometimes longer. Not in our control. |
| ⚠️ **X (Twitter)** | Publishing to X requires a **paid** X developer plan. Ask us about this before relying on it. |

## 10. Common questions — أسئلة شائعة

**Do you have my Instagram password?**
No. You log in on Instagram's own page and grant permission there. We never see
it. You can revoke that permission from Instagram at any time.

**Can my client see my other clients' posts?**
No. A Client user is locked to one brand.

**What happens if a post fails to publish?**
Nashr retries automatically. If it still fails, you are notified and can see the
reason — usually a disconnected account or something the platform rejected.

**Can I get my content out if I leave?**
Yes. Your posts, images and schedule are yours.

**Is my data backed up?**
Yes — nightly, encrypted, stored off the server. Ask us for current details.

**Where does my content go when I use the AI?**
Post text and your business profile are sent to an AI provider outside the
region to generate suggestions. Passwords, tokens, email addresses and phone
numbers are stripped out before anything is sent. If this matters for your
business, tell us and simply do not use the AI features — everything else works
without them.

**Is Nashr open source?**
Yes. Nashr is built on an open-source project called Postiz and is released
under the same licence (AGPL-3.0). You can see the full source code from the
"Licences & Source" link at the bottom of any page. This is a legal requirement
we take seriously, not a marketing line.

**Can I ask for a feature?**
Please do. You are one of a small number of first customers and there is no
roadmap so fixed that your feedback cannot change it. Snapchat, WhatsApp and a
mobile app are the three we are actively counting requests for.

## 12. Your first week — أول أسبوع

A realistic plan.

**Day 1** — Create your account. Fill in your business profile, including the
list of things you must never claim. Start the Instagram, Facebook and TikTok
approval applications with us. *(30–45 minutes.)*

**Day 2–3** — Connect whatever is already possible (LinkedIn, Telegram, Google
Business Profile). Invite your team. Decide who approves what.

**Day 4–5** — Write five posts as drafts. Run one all the way through the
approval flow so everyone has seen it work. Try the AI assistant in both Arabic
and English, and tell us what the Arabic reads like.

**Week 2** — Plan a full week on the calendar. Publish your first real post as
soon as a platform approval comes through, and watch it go out. Then tell us
what was confusing.

You are one of a very small group of first customers. Blunt feedback is more
useful to us than politeness.
